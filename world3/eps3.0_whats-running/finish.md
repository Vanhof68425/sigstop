# disconnect

Two processes on a machine that's supposed to be asleep. One we
could name. One we couldn't, until you watched.

`ckpt-sync`. A checkpoint is a model's saved state, everything it has
learned, frozen into files. Syncing checkpoints is what you do when
you want a copy of a model somewhere else. On an eval node. Out of
service. Every night at three.

You read the USER column like I asked. `harness`. Not a person. It's
the automated account that runs Apex's evals, the part of the system
that feeds a model its tests and writes down its scores. Harnesses
run things. That's their job. It probably means nothing.

I've been saying "probably" more than I like this week.

Here's what you did tonight, in operator terms: you found a process
by name, and you found one you didn't know to look for. Most
engineers can do the first. Almost none of them ever do the second,
because it requires admitting you don't know what's on your own
machine.

You didn't touch anything. Good. You're going to want to. Tomorrow
you'll learn how, and the first thing you'll learn is that almost
nothing you send a process is an order. It's a request.

Almost nothing.
