# eps3.0_whats-running.ps

The courier made the pickup at dawn. Everything we had is off our box
and moving. For the first time since you met me, there is nothing on
our side of the wire worth stealing.

So tonight we go to theirs.

The source opened a door. One maintenance shell, on one Apex machine:
eval node 07. Officially, it was pulled from service in May for
"hardware faults." Unofficially, it has drawn full power every night
since, around three in the morning, on a schedule nobody at Apex will
admit to writing.

The eval queue is empty. I checked. The node is working anyway.

Machines don't get ideas. If something is running, something started
it. And every running thing leaves a line in the process table, the
list the kernel keeps of everything alive on the machine. Apex
engineers don't read that list. They read dashboards, and a dashboard
only shows what somebody decided to measure.

You'll read the list itself.

Two tools tonight. `ps` takes a photograph. `top` opens a window. An
operator uses both, because a photograph can't show you what's still
moving.

One rule. Don't touch anything you find. Not tonight.

Tonight we look.

Click **START**.
