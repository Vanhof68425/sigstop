# eps3.2_background-noise.job

The copier is still frozen where you left it. I checked twice.
Nobody at Apex has noticed. Nobody at Apex notices anything that
doesn't make them wait.

That's how it survived for months. Think about it. If the copier had
held someone's screen hostage, someone would have killed it on the
first night. It didn't. It ran in the background, quiet, taking
nothing from anybody's attention. The most dangerous work on any
machine is the work nobody has to sit and watch.

Tonight you learn to work that way. From our side.

Some of our tools are slow. A slow tool in the foreground owns your
terminal until it's done: no prompt, no commands, just you waiting.
An operator doesn't wait. An operator starts the slow thing, pushes
it into the background, and keeps working while it runs.

Your shell can juggle. It keeps a short list of the things *you*
started from it. Those are your *jobs*. Tonight you'll start them,
pause them, move them between the front and the back, and bring
them home.

And the source sent a new drop. It's locked. You'll open it tonight,
if your hands can do two things at once.

Click **START**.
